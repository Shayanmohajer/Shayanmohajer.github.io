About

Researcher at the intersection of theory, learning, and systems

Shayan Mohajer Hamidi is a Postdoctoral Scholar in the Department of Electrical Engineering at Stanford University. He previously held a postdoctoral appointment at the University of Toronto and completed both his PhD and MASc in Electrical and Computer Engineering at the University of Waterloo.

His recent work spans LLM reasoning, autonomous agents, PEFT methods, diffusion models for inverse problems, federated learning, and optimization. He combines mathematical rigor with practical machine learning systems work across modern AI settings.

Research interests

Current areas of focus

LLM reasoning

Improving multi-step reasoning, planning, and structured decision-making in language models.

Autonomous agents

Strengthening the reasoning and planning capabilities of agentic AI systems.

PEFT and adaptation

Designing efficient fine-tuning methods, including low-rank and parameter-efficient adaptation.

Diffusion for inverse problems

Developing principled posterior sampling and manifold-aware diffusion approaches.

Federated learning

Studying fairness, communication efficiency, class imbalance, and wireless learning settings.

Optimization and information theory

Using mathematical tools to analyze and improve learning efficiency, robustness, and accuracy.
